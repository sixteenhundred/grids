# Grid. Brand Concept & Guidelines

## 1. Brand Idea

**Grid.** is the professional marketplace for photographers, cinematographers, videographers and drone pilots.

It helps clients find trusted visual talent fast, and helps creatives get booked, paid, reviewed and discovered without chasing messy DMs, unclear briefs or late payments.

The brand should feel like the meeting point between a production house, a premium creative network and a modern booking app.

**Core idea:**
**Visual talent, booked with confidence.**

**Alternative expression:**
**The production grid for modern visual work.**

## 2. Positioning

Grid is for businesses, agencies, brands, real estate companies, hotels, event organizers and private clients who need professional visual content without the friction of traditional sourcing.

For creatives, Grid is where serious photographers, cinematographers and drone pilots build trust, showcase work, package services, collaborate, learn and earn.

Grid should not feel like a cheap gig marketplace. It should feel curated, fast, protected and professional.

## 3. Mission

To make professional visual production easier to book, safer to manage and better for both clients and creatives.

## 4. Vision

A global network where any client can find verified visual talent in minutes, and every serious creative can run their business from one place.

### 4. Fair work, fair pay

### 5. One place to run the project

## 11. Product Story

Grid has two sides.

For clients, it is a fast way to hire trusted visual creators. They can search by category, city, budget and style, review portfolios, choose packages, sign contracts and fund escrow.

For creators, it is a business dashboard. They can showcase portfolios, set packages, receive bookings, apply to jobs, manage contracts, track escrow, collaborate with crew, upload media, join Academy courses, sell digital products and build reputation.

The product should feel like a premium creative marketplace with operational depth.

## 14. Logo Concept

The current wordmark **Grid.** is strong because it is short, confident and product-like.

The period gives it authority. It feels like a statement.

### Logo idea

A clean wordmark: **Grid.**

Possible symbol directions:

1. **Grid mark**
   A 3x3 grid where one cell becomes a camera frame, play icon or drone marker.

2. **Focus frame**
   Four corner brackets forming a viewfinder.

3. **Location grid**
   A map/grid intersection with a dot, suggesting nearby talent and Radar.

4. **Production tile**
   Modular squares representing creators, clients, projects and media assets.

### Recommended identity

Use **Grid.** as the primary logo. Use a minimal grid/focus symbol only as an app icon or favicon.

## 17. UI Style

### Shape language

Rounded, but not cute.

Recommended:

* Cards: 18 to 24 px radius
* Sheets: 28 px top radius
* Buttons: full pill radius
* Portfolio tiles: 14 px radius
* Avatars: circular

### Layout

Mobile-first.
Card-based.
Dark mode default.
Strong bottom navigation.
Large media previews.
Fast action buttons.

### Motion

Subtle, quick and functional.

Use motion for:

* Opening booking sheets
* Upload progress
* Escrow success
* Project stage progress
* Radar pulses
* Urgent job alerts

Avoid excessive animation.

## 24. Experience Principles

### 1. Show the work first

Portfolios should dominate the experience.

### 2. Make trust visible

Do not hide escrow, contracts or verification.

### 3. Keep actions close

Book, apply, message, upload, sign and deliver should always be nearby.

### 4. Separate client and creator flows

Clients think in outcomes. Creators think in jobs, rates and reputation.

### 5. Make money feel clear

Budgets, day rates, fees, escrow and releases should be transparent.

### 6. Turn production chaos into stages

Planning, shoot, edit, fixes and delivery should be easy to follow.

## 26. Brand Do’s and Don’ts

### Do

Use strong portfolio imagery.
Lead with trust and quality.
Keep copy short.
Use dark, premium surfaces.
Make escrow and contracts visible.
Respect creative professionals.
Show both sides of the marketplace.

### Don’t

Sound like a cheap gig app.
Overpromise instant perfection.
Hide fees or payment terms.
Use too many decorative icons.
Make the brand too playful.
Use generic stock photos.
Treat photographers, filmmakers and drone pilots as interchangeable commodities.
